About Me

I am a PhD Research Scholar at IIT Kharagpur working on Guidance, Navigation, and Control. My research focuses on autonomous rendezvous and docking of satellite, trajectory optimization, and advanced nonlinear control techniques. My goal is to develop intelligent and robust control frameworks that enable autonomous satellite operations in complex and uncertain space environment. I have presented research at leading international conferences, including the International Astronautical Congress (IAC), and actively contribute to the fields of spacecraft control, guidance systems, and autonomous space mission design.
